About Us

The Middle-Ground Network MN is a grassroots initiative building inclusive, action-oriented communities across Minnesota. Our mission is to foster dialogue, collaboration, and shared projects among individuals and groups who value thoughtful engagement over polarization.

Local Nodes

Local nodes are community-led chapters where members connect, organize, and take action. Each node maintains autonomy while staying connected to the larger Middle-Ground Network MN.
